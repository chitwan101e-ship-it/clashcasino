Wallet and game balances

A checkout receipt tells you about a purchase. A game card shows credits on that platform. Use the examples below to work out where to look for the transaction you just made.

Which balance are you looking at?

Clash Casino wallet

Start here when checking a purchase or looking at funds available to move. Open the wallet history and find the entry for the amount you expected.

Individual game

Start here after sending credits to a platform. Check the same game card you selected for the transfer. A change in one game’s balance won’t explain the balance shown on another game’s card.

The platform name on the card matters when titles overlap. Epic Summer can appear in both Juwa and Juwa 2.0, so a transfer to one card does not change the balance on the other. Open the card named in the wallet entry, then compare that screen with the history row.

Before confirming a purchase

Use the wallet inside your signed-in account. Review the selected amount, the payment method, and the total displayed at checkout. After submitting, keep the confirmation or reference number so you can match it to the transaction in your history.

If the checkout screen closes before you see a result, check for an existing transaction before submitting the payment again. Use the current checkout screen and Clash Casino’s terms for the methods, amounts, and conditions available to your account.
