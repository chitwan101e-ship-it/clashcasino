14 questions

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Open Clash Casino login. Use the recovery option there if you’ve forgotten the password. For a new account, the account setup guide walks through registration and verification.

Where is my verification email?

Check the email address on the registration screen, then search your inbox and spam folder for the verification message. If you request a fresh code, use the newest message. Return to the verification screen to enter it.

Why do I see a different login after opening a game?

Your Clash Casino login opens your account and wallet. A game may use its own platform credentials. Open the game card in your account and follow the access details for that platform.

Why are my wallet and game balances different?

The wallet shows funds held in Clash Casino. A game balance reflects credits held on that platform. Check the direction of the last transfer and the name of the game it went to. The balance guide shows where to look.

What should I check if a payment or transfer is pending?

Look up the original entry in your account history before trying again. Note the time, amount, destination, and reference number. If the status is unclear, give those details to support through your account.

Where do I check a cashout?

Look for the original request in wallet history. Review its current status, payout destination, and any instructions shown on the request. The transaction guide covers the difference between a cashout and moving credits back from a game.

Which payment methods and amounts can I use?

Open checkout in your account to see the methods, purchase amounts, and total currently offered to you. Review them before confirming.

Can I find a Juwa game if I only remember the cover?

Yes. Open the Juwa cover gallery and choose Slots, Fish games, or Keno to narrow the list. You can also type part of a title, such as “Buffalo,” then select the matching cover for a closer look.

Is Epic Summer in Juwa or Juwa 2.0?

It appears in both featured collections. Check the platform name above the game or on your account’s game card. The Juwa 2.0 article names other titles shared between the versions.

How can I tell a fish game from a reel game?

In a fish game, targets move while you aim. Reel games resolve a spin into a sequence of symbols. The game formats guide illustrates the difference using Deep Sea Predator and Fortune Lion.

Where does Orion Stars list keno?

Look under Table Games. The Orion Stars article explains how that category fits beside Fish Games, Slots, and Links.

Does Clash Arcade need my account details?

What information should I share with support?

For an account issue, describe the screen and exact message you saw. For a wallet issue, include the transaction reference and time. Keep passwords, verification codes, and full payment details private.
