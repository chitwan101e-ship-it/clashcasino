Start with a name you remember

If all you remember is a creature in the title, Milky Way’s list gives you a few leads. Ocean King, Golden Toad, Thunder Dragon, and King of Treasures all appear in the distributor’s selection. King of Treasures and Ocean King are two separate entries, an easy detail to miss if you search only for “King.”

Fish tables and reels

Milky Way includes fish-table and slot-style games. On a fish table you track a target as it moves. On reels, a spin ends with symbols lined up in columns. The same distributor also lists Lucky 777, Fruit Paradise, and Buffalo Thunder. For a particular title, the selection in your own lobby is the one you can actually open.

Recognizing Milky Way 777

You may see the name written as Milky Way, Milkyway, or Milky Way 777. The public landing page is mostly pictures and buttons. If you’re trying to recall a title before opening the platform, the distributor’s written list is easier to scan.

Two details worth remembering

When a name comes back only in pieces, keep both the creature and the second word. Thunder Dragon and Dragon Slayer both appear on the distributor’s list. Likewise, Ocean King and King of Treasures share a word but have different titles. Saving the full label alongside the Milky Way name makes it easier to find the same entry later.
