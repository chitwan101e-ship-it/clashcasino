Juwa 2.0

Which games do the two Juwas share?

Epic Summer appears in both collections. Lightning Respin and Goblin Bros are two names to look for when browsing Juwa 2.0.

The familiar names

Coming from the original Juwa, you may recognize Simple Dollar, Power Bucks, and Epic Summer. JuwaEmpire lists all three under both versions. Recognizing one of those covers tells you the game’s name, but it won’t tell you which version of Juwa you have open.

What stands out in the 2.0 selection

Lightning Respin, Goblin Bros, and Golden Horn appear in JuwaEmpire’s featured 2.0 selection. On the same page, Fortune Lion and Rainbow Riches appear in the original selection. If you’re trying to find a game again, the collection heading is as useful as the title.

Finding the right lobby

The two collections have separate play links on JuwaEmpire. Choose the link under the version named on your game account. If all you remember is the picture, the original Juwa cover gallery is a handy place to check names such as Fortune Lion before opening a lobby.
