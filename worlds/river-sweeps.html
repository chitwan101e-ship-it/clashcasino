A closer look at River Sweeps fishing

Fishing appears in two places on River Sweeps: the multiplayer games themselves and the fish tournament featured on River777.

What a fish table feels like

The first thing to watch on a fish table is the path of a target. A fish can cross your position while you’re choosing where to aim. River Sweeps describes multiplayer fishing, so several players can follow the action on the same table.

A game and a tournament

River777 places its Popular Games tiles near the top of the page. Farther down, a separate Fish Tournament feature lists scheduled battles. If you’re looking for a familiar game cover, start at Popular Games. If you’re looking for the event, the tournament listing is where River777 gives its schedule.

Beyond the fish table

River Sweeps also groups artwork under Rapid Link and Hold ’n’ Link. Those labels can help you retrace a game you saw earlier: a title shown in one of the Link groups belongs to a different collection from the fishing artwork shown here.

Finding a game you saw on River777

Start with what you remember seeing. If it was a single cover among a row of tiles, revisit Popular Games on River777. If the screen mentioned a battle time, look for Fish Tournament. A saved picture of the game tile or the event name gives you something specific to compare the next time the page layout changes.

Fishing versus Link games

River Sweeps uses fishing in two different places. The multiplayer fish games are the tables where several players follow moving targets. The Fish Tournament on River777 is a scheduled event, listed apart from the Popular Games tiles. Rapid Link and Hold ’n’ Link are different collections again. A title in one of those Link groups is not one of the fishing screens described above.

If you are comparing fish games across Clash Casino platforms, Fire Kirin shows Baby Octopus and Arc of Templar, and Juwa shows Deep Sea Predator and Happy Fishing. Same kind of table, different platform name on the game card. For the format itself, see the fish-game guide.
