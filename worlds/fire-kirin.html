Fire Kirin

Inside two Fire Kirin fish games

Baby Octopus sends you out over a coral reef. Arc of Templar puts a knight among sea monsters. Both belong to Fire Kirin’s fish arcade.

The reef in Baby OctopusA knight beneath the wavesWhere the reels come inRead the screen before picking a game

The reef in Baby Octopus

An octopus swims through schools of fish on the Baby Octopus artwork. The theme carries into the game: you follow creatures through the water and use nets and other tools to catch them. The moving target is what gives a fish table its arcade feel.

A knight beneath the waves

Arc of Templar keeps the underwater setting and borrows its cast from medieval fantasy. You play a Templar knight facing sea serpents and krakens. In the gameplay image, the action spreads across the middle of the table, with the controls along its edges.

Where the reels come in

Fire Kirin also has reel games. Buffalo 777 makes the change in format easy to see. Its screen is divided into columns of symbols, while the creatures in Arc of Templar move across a shared field.

Read the screen before picking a game

Look at the Buffalo 777 image alongside the fish-game screenshots. The letters and numbers occupy fixed spaces on the reels. In Arc of Templar, creatures overlap and move across the play area. That difference is a more reliable clue to the format than the animal on the cover: an animal theme can work just as well on reels as on a fish table.
