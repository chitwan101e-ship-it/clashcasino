Juwa

Three ways to play Juwa

Fortune Lion, Deep Sea Predator, and Buffalo Keno share a lobby. What you do after you open each game is quite different.

Start with the lionFrom reels to the fish tableRecognize them in the lobbyFind a cover when you remember only one detail

Start with the lion

Look for the red-and-white lion head with fireworks behind it. That’s Fortune Lion, a slot-style title in the original Juwa collection. The spin is one round of play, and the symbols settle before the next round begins. Snow Crystal Wilds and Reels & Wheels XL have their own unmistakable covers. Look for an icy blue seven on one, a multicolored wheel on the other.

From reels to the fish table

Deep Sea Predator trades the reels for moving targets. On a fish table, you choose where to aim while creatures cross the screen. The rhythm changes again in Buffalo Keno. There you pick numbers and follow the draw to see which ones match. If you’re looking for a particular kind of game, those three covers are an easy way to remember the difference.

Recognize them in the lobby

The clearest clue on Buffalo Keno is the word Keno beneath the buffalo. Deep Sea Predator has a blue, armored sea creature above its title. You can open any of the 57 covers in the Juwa gallery to inspect the lettering at a larger size.

Find a cover when you remember only one detail

If you remember a blue seven but have forgotten the title, open the Juwa gallery and choose Slots. Snow Crystal Wilds shows the seven against an icy background. If the clue is a buffalo, switch to Keno and search for “Buffalo” to bring up Buffalo Keno. Each cover opens at a larger size so you can read the smaller lettering.
